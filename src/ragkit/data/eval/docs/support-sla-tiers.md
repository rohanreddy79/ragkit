# Support Plans and SLAs

Helios offers three support tiers. Every customer gets Standard; Premium and Enterprise are purchased add-ons.

## Coverage

- Standard: business hours (08:00 to 18:00 local time), Monday to Friday, email and portal.
- Premium: 24x7 for P1 issues, business hours for everything else; phone, email, and portal.
- Enterprise: 24x7 for P1 and P2, a named technical account manager, and quarterly service reviews.

## Response time targets

| Priority | Standard | Premium | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 (site down) | 1 hour | 30 minutes | 15 minutes |
| P2 (degraded) | 4 hours | 2 hours | 1 hour |
| P3 (minor) | 1 business day | 8 hours | 4 hours |
| P4 (question) | 3 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day |

Response time is the time to a human acknowledgement with a plan, not the time to resolution.

## Service credits

Enterprise customers receive a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee for each P1 response-time miss, up to 25% in a month. Credits are requested through the account manager within 30 days.

## Priorities

P1 means robots are stopped site-wide or the Beacon API is unavailable. P2 means throughput is reduced by more than 25% or a single zone is stopped. P3 covers individual robots or non-urgent defects. P4 is a question or a feature request.
