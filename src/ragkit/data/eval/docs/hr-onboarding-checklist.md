# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

## Before day one

IT ships a laptop to arrive two business days before the start date. The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy from a different team and writes a 30/60/90-day plan in the shared onboarding template. HR sends the offer paperwork and the I-9 verification link.

## Day one

New hires pick up their badge at the front desk (or receive a digital badge if remote), sign in to Okta with the temporary password sent by IT, and enrol their laptop in MDM. The buddy walks through Slack channels, the wiki, and the engineering handbook. Orientation with People Ops runs from 10:00 to 12:00 PT.

## First two weeks

Complete the mandatory trainings in Lattice Learn: security awareness, harassment prevention, and data privacy basics. Everyone also completes robot safety training before entering the lab or any customer site. Engineers should have a first pull request merged by the end of week two, even if it is small.

## First 90 days

Check in on the 30/60/90 plan at each milestone. At 90 days, the manager completes a short onboarding review in Lattice, and the new hire gives feedback on the onboarding process in an anonymous survey.

## Accounts to request

GitHub organisation membership, PagerDuty (engineers only), Expensify, Workday, and a Beacon sandbox API key. Request everything else through HelpDesk.
