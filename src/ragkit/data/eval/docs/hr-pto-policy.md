# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Helios Dynamics gives all full-time employees 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued at 1.67 days per month starting on your first day. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata based on scheduled hours. PTO covers vacation and personal days; sick leave is tracked separately and does not draw down your PTO balance.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests in Workday at least two weeks before the first day you plan to be away. Requests of five or more consecutive days need your manager's approval at least four weeks in advance so coverage can be arranged. Your manager should respond within three business days; unanswered requests can be escalated to your HR business partner.

## Carry-over and payout

Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next calendar year; anything above that is forfeited on January 1. Rolled-over days must be used by March 31. We do not pay out unused PTO except where required by local law, in which case the balance is paid with the final paycheck.

## Blackout periods

The Operations and Customer Support teams observe a blackout period from November 15 to December 31, when customer sites run peak volume. Exceptions require a director's sign-off. Engineering has no blackout period but should avoid overlapping absences within the same on-call rotation.

## Company holidays

Helios observes 11 paid company holidays per year, published each December for the following year. If a holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest weekday is observed. Holidays do not count against your PTO balance.
