# Pricing and Discount Approvals

## List pricing

Atlas robots are sold as a subscription per robot per month, which includes Relay, Beacon API access, Standard support, and firmware updates. Premium and Enterprise support are priced as a percentage of the subscription. List prices are in the pricing sheet in the Sales wiki and change at most once a year with 90 days' notice to customers.

## Discount authority

- Up to 10%: account executive.
- 10% to 20%: regional sales director.
- 20% to 30%: VP of Sales.
- Over 30%, or any non-standard payment terms: VP of Sales and CFO together.

Multi-year commitments earn a standard 5% per additional year, which does not count against the discount thresholds above.

## Pilots

A paid pilot of five robots for 90 days is the standard entry offer. Free pilots require VP of Sales approval and must convert to a subscription or be removed within 120 days.

## Non-standard terms

Any change to the master subscription agreement, including liability caps, data residency commitments, or custom SLAs, goes through Legal with a deal desk ticket. Promising roadmap features in a contract is not permitted; roadmap items can be referenced only as targets in a side letter approved by Product.

## Quotes

Quotes are generated in the CPQ tool and are valid for 30 days. Discounts above 20% require the justification field to be completed and are reviewed in the weekly deal desk meeting.
