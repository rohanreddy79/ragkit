# Procurement Policy

## Thresholds

- Under $5,000: purchase with a corporate card or expense claim; manager approval only.
- $5,000 to $25,000: a purchase order (PO) raised in Coupa with budget-owner approval.
- Over $25,000: a PO plus three competitive quotes, or a written sole-source justification approved by the CFO.
- Over $100,000: CFO approval and a contract reviewed by Legal.

## Software and SaaS

All software, including free tiers, is purchased through IT so licences are tracked and the vendor completes a security review. Do not expense SaaS subscriptions on a corporate card. Renewals are reviewed 60 days before the term ends.

## Vendor onboarding

New vendors complete the vendor onboarding form, provide tax and banking details through the secure portal (never by email), and pass a security review if they will handle Helios or customer data. Finance verifies banking changes by a phone call to a known contact before updating a vendor record.

## Payment terms

Standard terms are net 30 from receipt of a correct invoice referencing the PO number. Invoices without a PO number are returned. Early-payment discounts are taken when offered at 2% or better.

## Hardware and robot components

Robot components and lab equipment follow the same thresholds but are approved by the Hardware Engineering budget owner, and long-lead items can be forecast on a blanket PO for the quarter.
