# Travel and Expense Policy

Helios reimburses reasonable business expenses submitted through Expensify with an itemised receipt. Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase date; claims older than 60 days are rejected unless your director approves an exception.

## Meals

Meals while travelling are capped at $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved team events. Tips up to 20% are included within the daily cap.

## Flights and lodging

Book flights through the Navan travel portal. Economy class is standard; premium economy is allowed for flights over six hours, and business class requires VP approval. Hotels are capped at $250 per night in most cities and $350 per night in New York, San Francisco, London, and Tokyo. Book at least 14 days ahead when possible.

## Ground transport

Use rideshare or public transport. Rental cars are permitted when visiting customer sites outside major cities; choose the mid-size class or smaller and decline the rental company's insurance, which is covered by our corporate policy. Personal mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS rate.

## Approvals

Expenses under $500 are auto-approved after your manager's review. Anything over $500, or any single item over $1,000, needs a second approval from the budget owner. Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times a year; card statements still require receipts in Expensify.
