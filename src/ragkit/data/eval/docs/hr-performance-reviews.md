# Performance Review Cycle

Helios runs two performance cycles a year: a mid-year review in March and an annual review in September. Compensation changes and promotions are decided only in the September cycle; the March cycle focuses on feedback and goals.

## Timeline

- Week 1: self-review opens in Lattice; employees have ten days to complete it.
- Week 2: peer feedback. Each employee nominates three to five peers; managers can add reviewers.
- Week 3: manager assessments and calibration sessions by department.
- Week 5: results delivered in one-on-ones.

## Rating scale

Ratings use a five-point scale: 1 (below expectations), 2 (partially meets), 3 (meets), 4 (exceeds), 5 (far exceeds). Calibration sessions exist to make ratings consistent across teams; a rating of 5 requires a written justification reviewed by the department head.

## Promotions

Promotion packets are due three weeks before the September cycle opens. A packet includes the manager's nomination, two peer endorsements, and evidence against the level expectations in the engineering or business career ladder. Promotions to Staff level and above are reviewed by a cross-functional committee.

## Performance improvement

An employee rated 1 or 2 is placed on a 60-day performance improvement plan with weekly check-ins and written goals. HR must review the plan before it is shared with the employee.
