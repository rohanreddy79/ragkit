# Safety and Certification

Atlas robots are certified to ISO 3691-4 for driverless industrial trucks and carry UL certification for North America; CE marking is in progress for the EU launch. Each site is recertified annually by a Helios field safety engineer.

## Protective systems

- Safety-rated 2D LiDAR scanners front and rear create protective fields that slow the robot at 2 m and stop it at 0.5 m from an obstacle.
- Speed near people is limited to 0.3 m/s within 1 m, enforced by the safety controller independently of the navigation software.
- Two physical emergency stop buttons, one on each side, cut drive power immediately. A site-wide emergency stop is available from the Relay dashboard and from wall-mounted buttons at each aisle entrance.
- Light curtains protect charging dock areas and packing stations.

## Site requirements

Floors must be level within a 1% grade with marked robot lanes. Aisles need a minimum width of 1.2 m for mixed robot and pedestrian traffic. Human-only zones are configured in Relay and physically marked. Operators and anyone entering a robot zone complete robot safety training, which must be renewed annually.

## Incident reporting

Any contact between a robot and a person, even without injury, is a SEV1 incident. Stop the robot, keep the scene unchanged, export the diagnostic bundle, and notify the Helios safety team within one hour.

## Recertification

Annual recertification covers protective field tests, emergency stop tests, and a review of site map changes. Changes to aisles or zones between certifications require a safety review before robots run in the changed area.
