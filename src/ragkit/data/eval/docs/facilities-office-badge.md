# Office Access and Facilities

## Hours and badges

The San Jose headquarters is open to employees 24 hours a day with a badge; reception is staffed from 08:00 to 18:00. Badges are issued at the front desk on your first day. Report a lost badge to facilities@helios.example immediately so it can be deactivated; replacements are free the first time and $25 afterwards.

## Visitors

Visitors sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge, and must be escorted at all times. Customer visits to the robotics lab are booked at least two days ahead through the facilities calendar so the lab can be put in a safe demonstration state.

## Robotics lab

The lab is a robot operating zone. Entering it requires completed robot safety training, closed-toe shoes, and a high-visibility vest from the rack by the door. After 18:00 and on weekends, a second trained person must be present whenever a robot is powered.

## Parking and transport

Parking is free in the north lot; EV chargers are available on a first-come basis with a two-hour limit. Secure bike storage is inside the loading dock entrance. Commuter transit passes are subsidised at 50% through the benefits portal.

## Facilities requests

Report broken equipment, temperature problems, or supply needs in HelpDesk under "Facilities". Desks are unassigned except for teams with lab equipment; book a desk in the Envoy app for in-office days.
