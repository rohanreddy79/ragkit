# Data Retention Schedule

This schedule defines how long Helios keeps each category of data and who owns deletion. Data is deleted or anonymised at the end of the retention period unless it is subject to a legal hold.

## Customer and fleet data

- Robot telemetry and task history: 13 months in the customer's site database, then aggregated to monthly statistics kept for the life of the contract.
- Robot camera images used for item verification: 7 days, then deleted; images are never used for other purposes without customer consent.
- Customer account and billing records: the duration of the contract plus 7 years.

## Operational data

- Application logs: 30 days hot, one year cold (see the logging guide).
- Security logs and audit trails: 2 years.
- Backups: 35 days of daily backups; a monthly backup is kept for one year.

## People data

- Employee records: 7 years after employment ends.
- Candidate applications: 12 months after the hiring decision, unless the candidate consents to a longer talent-pool retention.

## Deletion requests

Individuals may request deletion of their personal data. Requests go to privacy@helios.example and are completed within 30 days, with a written confirmation. Customer requests on behalf of their staff follow the data processing agreement.

## Legal holds

Legal may suspend deletion for data relevant to litigation or an investigation. Holds are recorded in the legal hold register and lifted in writing.
