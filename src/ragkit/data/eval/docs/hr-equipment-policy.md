# Laptops and Equipment

## Standard issue

The default laptop is a MacBook Pro 14-inch with 32 GB of memory. Members of the robotics firmware and perception teams receive a ThinkPad running Ubuntu because our firmware toolchain targets Linux. Each employee may also request one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, and a headset.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops are refreshed every three years, or earlier if a hardware failure cannot be repaired within five business days. Request a refresh through HelpDesk; IT will ship the new device with a prepaid return label for the old one.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 24 hours through HelpDesk or by paging the IT on-call line. IT will remotely wipe the device through MDM. If the device may have contained customer data, the Security team opens an incident under the security incident response process.

## Returning equipment

Departing employees return all company equipment within 10 days of their last day using the shipping kit IT sends. Unreturned equipment is invoiced at depreciated value.

## Personal devices

Personal laptops and phones may not access production systems or customer data. Slack and email on personal phones are permitted if the phone is enrolled in MDM and has a passcode.
