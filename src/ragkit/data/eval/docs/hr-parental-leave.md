# Parental Leave

Helios Dynamics offers paid parental leave to employees who become parents through birth, adoption, or foster placement. Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave; secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks. The benefit applies to all full-time employees with at least six months of continuous service on the date the leave begins.

## Scheduling

Leave may be taken in one continuous block or split into up to three segments, as long as all leave is used within 12 months of the birth or placement. Tell your manager and your HR business partner at least 30 days before your intended start date, or as soon as practical for an unexpected placement. Submit the request in Workday under "Leave of Absence".

## Pay and benefits during leave

Pay continues at 100% of base salary through regular payroll. Health, dental, and vision coverage continue unchanged, and equity vesting is not paused. Bonus eligibility is prorated for the portion of the year on leave.

## Returning to work

Employees return to the same role or an equivalent one. A four-week phased return at a 60% schedule and full pay is available on request. Short-term disability for birthing parents runs concurrently with the first weeks of parental leave; the HR team coordinates both claims so you only file once.

## Questions

Email people-ops@helios.example or book time with your HR business partner. State leave laws may provide additional protections; where they conflict with this policy, the more generous provision applies.
