# Product Roadmap 2026

This page summarises the committed themes for 2026. Dates are targets, not promises; customer communication goes through account managers only.

## H1 themes

- Atlas Lift: a variant of Atlas with a 30 kg payload and a scissor lift reaching 2.4 m shelves, targeted for pilot customers in June.
- Relay multi-site: one console view across sites, with cross-site task reporting. Fleet operations remain local to each site.
- Beacon GraphQL: a GraphQL endpoint alongside the REST API for customers who want fewer round trips; REST remains the primary interface.

## H2 themes

- EU launch: CE marking for Atlas and a Frankfurt region for the Helios cloud, with data residency for EU customers.
- Predictive maintenance: battery and drive-train health scoring from fleet telemetry, with proactive part replacement.
- Mixed fleets: Relay support for third-party conveyors and pallet movers through a partner integration SDK.

## Not planned this year

Outdoor operation, humanoid form factors, and a consumer product. Requests in these areas should be logged in the product feedback board, not promised to customers.

## How priorities are set

Product reviews priorities quarterly with the Sales, Support, and Engineering leads. Inputs are customer commitments, support ticket volume by feature, and engineering capacity from the capacity plan.
