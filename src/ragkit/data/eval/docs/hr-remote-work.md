# Hybrid and Remote Work

Helios Dynamics is a hybrid company. Employees based near an office are expected in the office three days a week, with Tuesday through Thursday as core in-office days so teams overlap. Monday and Friday are flexible.

## Fully remote roles

A role may be designated fully remote with approval from the VP of the department. Fully remote employees must maintain at least four hours of overlap with Pacific Time working hours (09:00 to 17:00 PT) and visit their home office once per quarter; travel for those visits is covered under the expense policy.

## Home office stipend

Every employee receives a $500 annual home office stipend, reset each January, for desks, chairs, monitors, and similar equipment. Items over $150 must be submitted through Expensify with a receipt. Co-working memberships are reimbursable up to $300 per month for fully remote employees instead of the stipend, not in addition to it.

## Working from another location

You may work from a location other than your registered address for up to 30 days per year without approval, provided you remain in a country where Helios has a legal entity. Longer stays or other countries require written approval from HR and Legal because of tax and employment-law exposure.

## Equipment and security

Remote employees must use a company-managed laptop enrolled in MDM and connect through the Tunnel VPN when accessing production systems. Public Wi-Fi is acceptable only with the VPN active.
